# Source registry

This registry records where each medicine-data claim came from and whether the source may legally and technically be reused.

## Rules
1. A source being official does not automatically mean its data may be copied, bulk-downloaded, or republished. Check terms, licence, copyright, API conditions, and any written permission.
2. Do not treat a search-engine snippet, pharmacy listing, social post, or unattributed spreadsheet as authoritative evidence of registration, approved indications, dosage, or current price.
3. Record the exact source/document and the fields it supports. One source rarely substantiates every field.
4. Keep price evidence separate from clinical evidence. Record the pack size, EGP price, effective/checked date, and geographic or channel limitations where supplied.
5. If a source cannot be accessed or its reuse permission is unclear, mark it accordingly and do not ingest its data.
6. Every record requires a qualified clinical review before publication.
